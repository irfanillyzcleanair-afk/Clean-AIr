**On-image headline:** Photocatalytic oxidation, explained in 3 steps.
```text
How does ductFIT treat air? With a process inspired by nature: photocatalytic oxidation (PCO).

2️⃣ UV light activates a catalyst surface
```

## Day 09 · Tue 10 Nov · Education · All
**Image:** `posts/day-09-explainer.jpg`  
**On-image headline:** What is PM2.5?
```text
PM2.5 = particulate matter 2.5 micrometres or smaller. For scale, a human hair is roughly 50–70 micrometres wide.

Common sources indoors and out:
• Dust and sand events
• Traffic and construction
• Cooking and frying
• Bakhoor and candles

You can't see it, so the only way to know your levels is to measure. Save this for later 🔖
```
