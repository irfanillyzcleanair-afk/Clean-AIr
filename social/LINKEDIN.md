## Day 04 · Thu 05 Nov · All
**Image:** `posts/day-04-steps.jpg`

```text
How does ductFIT treat air? Photocatalytic oxidation, in three steps:

1. Air carrying oxygen and water vapour moves through the unit, using a built-in fan or the building's HVAC airflow.
2. UV light activates a catalyst surface.
3. The reaction produces low levels of reactive molecules that act on selected contaminants while the unit operates.

The same principle runs across the range, from in-duct units to standalone room units.
```

## Day 09 · Tue 10 Nov · All
**Image:** `posts/day-09-explainer.jpg`

```text
PM2.5 in one minute:

• Particles 2.5 micrometres or smaller, roughly 30 times thinner than a human hair
• Common sources: dust and sand events, traffic, construction, cooking, incense
• Invisible, so the only way to know indoor levels is to measure them

If your building reports on wellbeing, PM2.5 is usually the first number people ask about.
```
