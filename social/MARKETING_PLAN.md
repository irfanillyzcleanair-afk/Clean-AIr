# CleanAir growth plan: offices, villas and government

Goal: turn Instagram attention into **site consultations and quotations** from three buyer groups.
Instagram builds awareness and trust. Most B2B and government deals will close through
LinkedIn, direct outreach, WhatsApp and tenders, so this plan covers all of them.

## 2. Instagram (30-day plan in this folder)

- **Posting:** 1 feed post a day at ~10:45 Dubai time (office audience is online mid-morning). Weekday B2B content, weekend villa/family content works best; the calendar already mixes both.
- **Content mix:** 33% education, 30% product, 20% audience-specific, 17% trust / engagement / conversion.
- **Stories (daily, 2–3 frames):** reshare the day's post, add a poll or question sticker, and a link sticker to WhatsApp.
- **Reels (2–3 a week, filmed on a phone):** these reach new people far better than images.
  1. Install time-lapse of a ductFIT Vent in an HVAC duct
  2. KleanSense dashboard: readings before and after a meeting (real data only)
  3. "Unboxing" ductFIT Mobile in a villa bedroom
  4. 15-second explainer: "What is PM2.5?" with the post as the background
  5. Founder / engineer answering one customer question per week
- **Profile setup (@cleanairme_):** switch to a Business account, category "Air purifier supplier" or "Business service", bio:
  `Air purification & indoor air quality monitoring 🇦🇪 | Offices · Villas · Government | ductFIT & KleanSense | Book a site visit 👇`
  Add WhatsApp button, the website link, and Highlights: *Products · Offices · Villas · Government · How it works · Contact*.
- **Engagement routine (20 minutes a day):** reply to every comment and DM within 1 hour; comment thoughtfully on posts from Dubai facility managers, interior designers, real-estate developers and FM companies.
- **Keyword replies:** Day 29 asks people to comment "AIR". Reply to each with a DM and log them as leads.

- **Use the Instagram QR code** (@cleanairme_) on business cards, quotations, email signatures, product boxes, exhibition stands and installation stickers so every customer you meet can follow you.

## 4. LinkedIn: the main channel for offices and government

- Create a **Clean Air Wellness & Hygiene company page** and reuse the same 30 designs (they work at 4:5 on LinkedIn too).
- The founder / sales lead posts 2–3 times a week from a personal profile: short lessons from site visits, IAQ facts, project photos.
- Weekly outreach: connect with 20 facility managers, FM company directors, HR leads and government facilities engineers in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, then send a short message offering a 20-minute technical presentation.

## 5. Government: getting on the supplier lists

Government entities buy through formal procurement, so social media alone won't win these deals.

1. Register as a supplier on the relevant procurement portals (for example the Dubai Government eSupply portal and the Abu Dhabi Government procurement portal; check each entity's current supplier registration page). Make sure the trade licence, VAT certificate and product data sheets are ready.
2. Prepare a **technical submittal pack**: product data sheets, installation method statements, maintenance plan, warranty terms, and the manufacturer's test reports or certifications **you actually hold**.
3. Approach **FM contractors** (companies that run government buildings) as partners; they often specify equipment for their clients.
4. Offer a **pilot**: one floor or one service centre with KleanSense monitoring before and after. Real data from a pilot is the strongest sales tool you can have.
5. Exhibit or visit trade events where facility and government buyers attend (FM, HVAC, smart-city and sustainability shows in Dubai).

## 6. Villas: partner channels

- **Interior designers and fit-out companies**: offer a referral commission; they decide what goes into a villa.
- **Real-estate agents and property managers** for luxury villa communities.
- **Google Business Profile**: create or claim "Clean Air Wellness & Hygiene", add photos, products and the WhatsApp number, and ask every customer for a Google review.

## 9. Rules for every post (keep the brand trustworthy)

- No medical or health guarantees ("kills 99% of viruses", "prevents illness") unless you have the test report and the right to publish it.
- Only publish certifications, test results and client names you have in writing.
- Use real photos of real installations whenever you can; ask clients' permission first.
- Government clients: never post their building or name without written approval.
