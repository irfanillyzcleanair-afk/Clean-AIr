# 10 Reels you can film on a phone

Reels reach people who don't follow you yet, so they are the fastest way to grow @cleanairme_.
Post 2–3 a week alongside the daily image posts.

**How to film**
- Hold the phone vertically (9:16). Film in daylight or a bright room, and wipe the lens first.
- Keep each clip 2–4 seconds. Short cuts hold attention.
- Put the hook as text on screen in the first second. Many people watch with the sound off.
- Add captions with Instagram's **Captions** sticker, or with the CapCut app.
- Use a trending instrumental sound at low volume under the voiceover.
- Only show real readings, real installations and real places. Get written permission before filming at a client's site, and never film a government building without approval.
- Cover image: use the matching post from `social/posts/` (named in each script).
- End every Reel with the same last frame: the logo, **@cleanairme_** and **WhatsApp +971 50 116 9833**.
